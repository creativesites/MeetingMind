MeetingMind Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · MeetingMind for Android, published by Craftflow Technologies

MeetingMind records and organises your meetings, lectures, sermons and notes. It is built to keep your material on your phone. This policy explains what stays on your phone, what leaves it, when, and why.

The short version

  • Your recordings, transcripts, notes and AI-written summaries are stored on your phone. MeetingMind has no account for the core app and does not upload your library.
  • In Offline mode (the default), recording, transcription and AI features run on your phone and nothing is uploaded.
  • If you turn on Internet mode, the audio or text for the task you ask for is sent to a cloud AI provider to do that task.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the app. We do not sell your data.

What stays on your phone

Audio recordings, transcripts, notes, summaries, decisions, action items, tasks, Bible study, devotionals, practice cards and your settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. They are excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup and device-transfer. You can delete any of them in the app, and uninstalling the app removes them.

When something leaves your phone

Cloud AI (Internet mode only)

When Internet mode is on and you use a cloud feature, such as transcribing a recording, writing notes from it, asking a question, making notes from a public YouTube video, or having a note read aloud with a cloud voice, the audio or text needed for that request is sent to:

  • Google Gemini: directly, if you entered your own Gemini API key, or through MeetingMind's AI service if you use the built-in allowance or a MeetingMind plan.
  • DeepSeek: for some text-only requests when Gemini is unavailable.

MeetingMind's AI service passes your request to the provider and returns the answer. It does not store your audio, transcripts or notes. It keeps only usage counters (seconds of cloud audio and the number of AI actions per month) against an anonymous identifier, to apply your plan's allowance. Notes you mark private, and Work content you mark confidential, are not sent to the cloud voice or included in AI reports.

The AI provider processes the request under its own terms. Under Google's terms for its free Gemini services, content may be used to improve Google's products and may be reviewed by people. Content sent through paid services is not used that way. If you use your own key, the terms of your Google account apply.

YouTube links

When you make notes from a YouTube video, the link to that public video is sent to Google Gemini to produce the transcript and notes. MeetingMind does not download the video. The note records the video's title, channel and link so it is properly credited.

Plans and purchases

If you buy or restore a plan, Google Play handles the payment and RevenueCat records which plan is active. MeetingMind never receives your payment details. An anonymous RevenueCat user id is used to check your plan.

Circles (optional groups)

The first time you create or join a Circle, the app signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication, which gives you a random user id. If you choose to link a Google account, your Google name, email address and profile photo are shared with Firebase to identify you in your Circles. MeetingMind's Circles service (hosted on Cloudflare and Google Firestore) keeps your Circle membership, role, the display name you chose, and what you post or share in a Circle. Your recordings, notes and transcripts are never sent to Circles unless you share them there yourself.

Reporting AI-written content

If you report a passage that AI wrote, the report sends the flagged passage, your reason and optional comment, which feature wrote it, the AI model and the app version. It sends no account, note title, transcript or recording. Reports about private notes, on-device mode or confidential work are never uploaded; you can copy them instead. We use reports to review and improve AI output. Your IP address is used only as a scrambled (hashed) value to limit abuse and is not stored with the report.

Downloads

The app downloads AI model files and Bible translations you choose to install. These downloads send nothing about you beyond what any web request does.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record, only when you start a recording.
  • Notifications: to show recording, playback and processing status.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep a recording or processing job running when the screen is off.
  • Internet: for the downloads, Internet-mode AI, plans and Circles described above.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location or your phone's general storage. Files are imported only through the system file picker, one file at a time, when you choose them.

Children

MeetingMind is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your phone: delete items in the app, or uninstall it.
  • Circles: leaving or deleting a Circle, or deleting your Circles account in the app, removes your membership records. To ask us to delete Circles data, email creativesites263@gmail.com.
  • AI usage counters are kept per calendar month for billing and abuse prevention.
  • AI reports are kept while they are useful for improving AI output, then deleted.

Security

Data sent off your phone travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). An API key you enter is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read, and is sent only to the AI provider it belongs to.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold, or to object to how it is used. Because most of your data never leaves your phone, you control it directly in the app. For anything held by our services (Circles, AI reports, usage counters), email creativesites263@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change what MeetingMind does with data, we will update this page and its effective date, and tell you in the app when the change is significant.
