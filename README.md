## What's new: Personal becomes "Your garden"
- **Your garden**: Personal's own home and bottom-bar place. A painted scene that follows the time of day, in light and dark themes; the day's question; your practices as plants; the Seed jar and Practices.
- **The lantern**: answer the day's question out loud, privately. A follow-up question appears after 40 seconds. Saved as a private Journal note.
- **Practices**: plant something to practise (it also shows in Tasks); tend it, adjust it, let it bloom or let it go. No streaks; plants rest, never die.
- **Seed packets**: "Try this week" lines from talk and how-to notes, planted in one tap. Any note line can be planted from its menu.
- **Seed jar**: your ideas, and a "To sort" list that suggests a home for untitled memos.
- **Your sky**: reflections as constellations of recurring themes. "Zuri's read" (paid, on-device model only).
- **Today**: an arched window onto the garden.
- Database version 32 (practices). Older data migrates on first launch.

1. Download the APK on the phone (link above).
2. Open it and allow installing from this source if Android asks.
3. It installs as **MeetingMind Dev** (`com.craftflowtechnologies.meetingmind.dev`), beside the tester app, with its own data.
