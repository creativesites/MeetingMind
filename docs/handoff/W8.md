## Built

Numbers follow the brief's W8 items.

1. **`core/work/Pulse.kt`** is pure queries, with no model.
   - `attention(now, limit = 4, …)` ranks in the brief's order:
     1. My commitments and tasks that are overdue or due today, oldest first. A task that already has an open commitment isn't counted twice.
     2. `PROPOSED` decisions, with those on a project that has a meeting today first.
     3. `THEIRS` commitments that are overdue or open five days or more.
     4. Quiet people and organisations with open items and no meeting for `quietDays`. A person whose organisation already has a row doesn't get a second.
   - `changesSince(t)` groups `item_events` by project, then organisation, then person. It renders specific sentences, never counts:
     - "Ana committed to API docs by Fri"
     - "Launch moved Oct 14 → Oct 21"
     - "Ana delivered X"
     - "Answered: Q — A"
     - "Decision changed: “old” → “new”"
     - Reviewed flags, deletions and link changes aren't news, so they don't appear.
   - `today(events)` returns calendar lines with open counts from the last meeting. It also says whether it's a first meeting.
   - `pulseSeenAt` is in `UserPreferences`. The Pulse marks itself read after two seconds on screen.
     - "Since …" is fixed for the session at the last read. If nothing has been read yet, it falls back to 24 hours ago.
     - The label reads "Since earlier today", "Since yesterday", a weekday, or a date.
2. **The Professional home.** The briefing card's body is now `PulseCard`, in `feature/work/PulseCard.kt`, with the same gradient.
   - Each attention row carries its actions: ▶ Evidence, Nudge (the existing `NudgeSheet`), Prepare, Open.
   - "Ask about my work" opens the Ask sheet on a new `LibraryAssistantViewModel.work` session.
   - **Cold start:** with no items, the card shows today's calendar lines ("First meeting with …"). With no calendar either, it shows "Start with one conversation", Record and Use a template.
   - Every other section of the home is untouched.
3. **The context page.** `ContextScreen.kt` is one screen for `PERSON`, `ORG` and `PROJECT`, with `Routes.context(type, id)`. `WORK_PERSON` and `PROJECT` open it, and their route strings are unchanged.
   - The existing `WorkPersonScreen` and `ProjectScreen` are its body. I added optional `extraTop` and `extraBottom` slots, so nothing they did was lost.
   - **Header:** You owe, They owe, Open, Decided, Next, and **Prepare for conversation**.
   - **Below the body:** decision history (the supersede chain, e.g. "WordPress is the source of truth (Sep 29), replacing Headless CMS (Sep 12)"), risks, and a timeline of `item_events` plus meetings with a **This month** filter.
   - **Organisation pages** have editable domain(s), description, URLs and properties, and list their projects.
   - **Project pages** have members (`project_members`), status, dates and custom properties.
4. **Prepare v1** (database only): `core/work/Prepare.kt` builds a `PrepPack` for an event, person, organisation or project.
   - Last meeting with a real quote and its time (the latest decision or commitment said).
   - What you owe, what they owe, decisions in force over three months, and questions to settle.
   - A suggested agenda: open items and proposed decisions only, ranked overdue → decide → they owe → questions → I owe, capped at 8.
   - `PrepareSheet` is reachable from Pulse rows and Pulse's today lines, the schedule rows on the Professional home, context pages, and the header of a work note made from a calendar event.
5. **Saved-filter chips** (`SavedViews.kt`, `SavedFilterRow`), answered from the database: What have I promised? · Who's waiting on me? · Active decisions (3 months) · What changed this month? They are on the Work space and on context pages, where the answer is scoped to that page.

Also closed a W7 gap: `WorkPeople.merge` now moves items' owner, counterparty, organisation and person links to the surviving person.

## Not built or changed

- **No new schema.** W8 adds no tables or columns, so there is no migration and no new schema JSON.
- **Prepare's model prose** ("Last time" line, agenda phrasing) is W10.
- **Scoped Ask from a context page** is W10. "Ask about my work" is the whole-library Ask for now.
- **"Who's waiting on me?"** groups by the counterparty when it's known. Since nothing sets counterparties yet, it falls back to the other participants of the meeting the promise was made in, then to an ungrouped list.
- **Backfill events are dated to the recording.** History that comes across on upgrade carries the recording's date, so it doesn't read as news in "what changed".
- **`WorkAllScreen`'s People tab** and the older person and project lists are unchanged.
- **The old Up-next tile in the Professional home's briefing card** (event title, "Last time" line, Record and Notes buttons) is gone, replaced by Pulse's today lines with Prepare and Record.

## How to see it

- **Pulse:** Home → Professional home, the top card. With no items it offers to record or use a template.
- **Prepare:** tap Prepare on a Pulse row, on a calendar line or schedule row, on a context page, or on a work note made from a calendar event.
- **Context page:** tap a person, organisation or project anywhere (or a Pulse row's Open). Try Prepare for conversation, an organisation's About → Edit, a project's Details and Members, and the Timeline's This month chip.
- **Saved filters:** the chips under the stats row in the Work space, and under the header on a context page.
- **Definition of done:** the Pulse and saved-filter tests run with no model and no network.

## Risks / questions for the orchestrator

- **Pulse recomputes on every item or event change** (a `MAX(updatedAt) + event count` query). That is fine at this scale, but I haven't measured it on a large history.
- **`PulseCard` keeps the briefing's fixed dark gradient** in both themes (tokens from `Briefing.*`), as the brief said.
- **A person page uses the same `ContextScreen` body** as before, and the old menu items (rename, merge, contact) are unchanged.
- **Person-page semantics:** "They owe" counts only that person's promises. "You owe" counts my promises linked to them as a participant. That was my call.
- **Nothing here uses AI**, so no privacy gate is needed yet.
