## Built

Numbers follow the brief's W10 items.

1. **`briefs` and `memory_stories`** in schema 20 (`core/database/Briefs.kt`, `MIGRATION_19_20`). `briefs` keeps the model's cited prose. It never stores structure, which is always read from items. `memory_stories` is keyed by (entity type, entity id, month).
2. **`core/work/ContextPack.kt`.** A scoped, bounded bundle for a meeting, a person, organisation or project, or all of Work, optionally over a time range.
   - It holds items (capped at 60, most pressing first), one evidence quote per item (trimmed to 200 characters), up to 8 meeting summaries (400 characters each), and up to 20 recent change sentences.
   - `ids` is everything a sentence may cite. `render()` is the prompt text, with each fact led by its id.
   - `sensitive` comes from `PackPrivacy`, whose production version (`DevicePackPrivacy`) asks `WorkPrivacy` about every recording used, and also checks a confidential person, organisation or project.
   - Briefs, Prepare's prose, monthly stories and scoped Ask all read through it.
3. **`core/work/Brief.kt`.**
   - **Structure, from the database:** what changed, decisions (three months), you owe, they owe, risks, open questions, next 7 days, decisions required, and the evidence appendix.
   - **Status and progress from counts:** Attention if anything is overdue, a decision is still proposed, or a risk is open. Progress is completed commitments over all commitments.
   - **Variants:** meeting, client (organisation), project status, relationship (person) and weekly.
   - **The model writes only two things:** the executive picture and the recommended next conversation, as JSON sentences that each cite ids. `BriefGrounding.keep` drops a sentence with no citation, or with any citation that isn't in the pack.
   - **Cache:** the prose is kept in `briefs` and reused until an item or its log changes. It is re-checked against today's pack on every read. Refresh always asks again.
   - **No model:** the brief renders with no prose, and nothing is stored.
   - The prompt starts with `TranscriptToolPrompts.FIDELITY_CONTRACT`.
4. **`BriefScreen`** (`Routes.BRIEF`, `Routes.brief(kind, id)`).
   - Sections show at once from the database, and the prose fills in when it arrives.
   - Every line, and every citation in the prose, expands to its quote with the meeting, date and a ▶ that opens the recording at that moment.
   - **Share** sends the Markdown as text. **Export** saves PDF, Word or Markdown. **Refresh** asks the model again.
   - **The export path:** `BriefExport.toDocument` builds an `ExportDocument`, and `ExportManager.writeDocument` writes it.
     - Evidence gets a number, and lines and sentences carry `[n]` markers.
     - The appendix starts on a new page and quotes each with meeting, date and time and the position in the recording.
     - A status chip sits under the title.
     - Confidential material prints a confidentiality line first.
   - **The Brief PDF theme** (new `ExportTheme.BRIEF`): clean type, a rounded status chip (teal on track, amber for attention), small tracked section headings, and a page break before the appendix. This needed a small `PageBreak` block, handled in the Markdown, Word and PDF renderers.
   - **Entry points:** ✨ Brief on a work recording's detail, ✨ Create brief on context pages, and ✨ Create brief (weekly) in the Work space.
5. **Prepare** (the skill). `PrepareSheet` shows an optional ✨ "Last time" line and agenda wording from the model. Each is cited and grounded the same way, and each opens its evidence. Without a model, or with nothing to prepare from, there is no prose.
6. **Memory.**
   - **History view on context pages:** the counts header, **The story**, **Most important** and **Still open**.
   - Counts and lists come from the database and show at once.
   - The story is one cited paragraph per month. It is written when a month is missing or its item count has changed, and it is kept. A month the model can't cite is stored empty, so it isn't asked again until the count moves. A first open writes only the newest three stale months.
   - **Scoped Ask** (`ai/assistant/ScopedAsk.kt`, with `AskScope`). Search is limited to the scope's recordings and notes, through `note_people`, the project's notes and the recordings its items came from. The pack goes in ahead of the transcript chunks. An answer that cites nothing is not shown: the sources are shown instead, with a plain reason. With no model, the sources search found are listed.
   - **Pre-scoped from a context page:** an Ask button on the page opens the sheet with a scope chip that clears to "My work". The Pulse's "Ask about my work" now uses this scoped sheet too, replacing the assistant session I added in W8 (removed).
7. **Every model call goes through `WorkModels`** (`DeviceWorkModels`). Sensitive material only ever gets the local model. If none is installed, there is no prose, and the cloud transport isn't even asked whether it's configured.

## Risks / questions for the orchestrator

- **A device check is still owed** for the PDF: Robolectric has no native PDF writer, so the layout is drawn onto canvases and its text checked, but nobody has looked at the printed page.
- **The model calls cost tokens.** A brief asks once per data change, a first open of History writes up to three months, and Prepare asks each time it opens. All are skipped with no model, and the cache keeps repeat views free. I haven't measured latency on a device.
- **Cache staleness rule.** Brief prose is reused only if nothing in its pack changed since it was written. A change to something outside the pack (another project's item) doesn't refresh it, which is the intent.
- **Ask searches with one query per content word**, not the whole question, because the existing full-text index needs every word to match. This is an improvement over what the question would otherwise return, but it's coarser than the cloud Ask's model-expanded queries.
- **The scoped Ask and the existing assistant** are separate: the assistant sheet (used in Faith and in notes) is unchanged and still talks to the cloud transport directly. The work entry points no longer use it.
