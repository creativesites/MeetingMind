## Built

1. **Saved Views (`core/work/ViewQueries.kt` & `feature/work/`):**
   - **Kanban Board (`KanbanBoard.kt`):**
     - Three workflow columns: `To Do`, `In Progress`, `Done`.
     - Supports dragging / moving cards between columns:
       - Moving an item card calls `itemRepository.setStatus`, persisting the state change and writing an `ItemEventType.STATUS` record to `item_events`.
       - Commitment cards linked to a `taskId` automatically synchronize status to the underlying task.
       - Independent task cards update their `doneAt` timestamp in Room.
     - Automatically partitions open, active, and overdue cards. Overdue items are elevated to "In Progress" to highlight immediate operational attention.
   - **Timeline View (`TimelineView.kt`):**
     - Unified chronological view across meetings, decisions, risks, commitments, and dated tasks.
     - Partitioned into logical, non-empty time buckets: `Overdue`, `Today`, `This Week`, `Upcoming`, and `Past Activity`.
     - Interactive click-throughs to source notes or items.
   - **Risks View (`WorkAllScreen.kt` & `ViewQueries.kt`):**
     - Dedicated `WorkTab.RISKS` in the primary Work dashboard.
     - Severity badges (`HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`), origin meeting link, counterparty attribution, and resolution status.
     - Sorted deterministically with HIGH severity risks at the top.
   - **Project View Tab Integration (`ProjectScreen.kt`):**
     - Tab bar on Project Screen switching between `Overview`, `Kanban`, `Timeline`, and `X-Ray`.

2. **Professional X-Ray (`core/work/XRay.kt` & `feature/work/XRayView.kt`):**
   - Read-only project entity graph and cited explanation view.
   - 100% offline and deterministic: builds directly from the Room database (`items`, `tasks`, `meetings`, `project_members`, `people`) and existing `ContextPack` citations with zero neural model dependency.
   - Visual clusters for `People`, `Decisions`, `Risks`, `Meetings`, and `Tasks`.
   - Node-level metadata with citation quotes, linked meetings, and relationship edges (`INVOLVED_IN`, `DECIDED_IN`, `IDENTIFIED_IN`, `BELONGS_TO`, `ASSIGNED_TO`).
   - Summary panel detailing meeting counts, active collaborator counts, and open tasks.

3. **Domain & View Models (`WorkViewModel.kt`):**
   - Added `loadProjectKanban(projectId)`, `loadProjectTimeline(projectId)`, `loadProjectXRay(projectId)`, `loadAllRisks()`, and `moveKanbanCard(card, targetColumn)`.
   - Integrated with existing `ItemRepository` and `WorkDao`.

## Not built or changed

- **No dependency-editing Gantt or interactive whiteboard:** Per spec decision D9 and W14 boundaries, dependency-editing Gantt charts and canvas whiteboards are deliberately omitted to preserve mobile speed, single-column focus, and avoid desktop-paradigm bloat.
- **No external graph service or LLM requirement:** X-Ray runs entirely on-device from SQLite entities.

## How to see it

1. Open **Work** from the main bottom navigation.
2. Select any project (e.g. an existing project notebook) to enter `ProjectScreen`.
3. Use the view tab switcher at the top:
   - Tap **Kanban** to see columns (`To Do`, `In Progress`, `Done`), tap the column icon or move action to transition cards.
   - Tap **Timeline** to see chronological milestones from Past Activity through Upcoming.
   - Tap **X-Ray** to see the project entity graph, clusters, citations, and offline summary.
4. From the top-level **Work** screen, switch to the **Risks** tab to view organization- or project-level risks sorted by severity.
