## Built

Numbers follow the brief's W12 items.

1. **Work notifications** (`core/work/Rhythm.kt`, `AppNotifications.workNotice`, channel `meetmind_work_rhythm`).
   - **The window** (`WorkWindow`): working days and working hours from `WorkSettings`. Everything outside them is quiet. The weekly review is the one nudge allowed on a non-working day (the person chose that day for it), and it still waits for working hours.
   - **Morning line:** one line: today's meetings, what needs you, what's in the Inbox. On a wall-clock alarm (`DailyAlarms`), so it keeps its time of day.
   - **Prep:** `prepLeadMinutes` before an event with people MeetingMind already knows, opening the Prepare sheet for that event.
   - **"Starting now — record?"** Opt-in (off by default). It opens the recorder with the title and workflow filled in.
   - **Weekly review:** on the review day, with what slipped and what's waiting. Opens the review.
   - **Redaction:** for a sensitive profile (clinical, legal) every notification has counts only. No meeting title, no name, no matter. This is decided in `WorkNotices`, before anything is posted, and there's a test that reads what was actually posted.
   - **Re-checked when they fire:** the alarm or the 15-minute check runs `WorkWindow` again, so a delayed alarm or a changed setting can't speak outside working hours. Each nudge is sent once per occasion.
   - **Only for people who use Work:** nothing is scheduled unless the Work space is on.
   - **Settings:** Work settings → Rhythm: working days and hours, the four switches, and the review day. New `WorkSettings` fields (all defaulted, so old saved settings read as before): `notifyMorning`, `morningMinute`, `notifyPrep`, `notifyStartNow`, `notifyWeekly`, `weeklyReviewMinute`. Changing a setting reschedules straight away; a reboot or update reschedules too (`DailyAlarmResync`).
2. **Weekly Review** (`core/work/WeeklyReview.kt`, `feature/work/WeeklyReviewScreen.kt`, `Routes.WEEKLY_REVIEW`).
   - **The facts** are the same `WeekReview` the Today card uses, extended with an optional `WorkWeek` (meetings, decisions, promised, done, open questions, slipped). It was extended, not forked.
   - **The five questions:** what changed (the Pulse's change lines), what I finished, what slipped, who I'm waiting on and who is waiting on me, and which projects need attention.
   - **Next week's plan, in one pass:** each open item is Carry, Move to (Mon–Fri of next week), or Drop. All start as Carry, so doing nothing loses nothing. Carry keeps the item and moves only a passed date to next Monday; Move sets the day; Drop cancels a commitment, drops a question, closes anything else. Every change is written to the item's history, and a linked task follows its new date.
   - **Optional weekly brief:** "✨ Create brief" (the entry point W10 deferred), plus "Weekly review" in the Work space.
3. **The Work tab.** With `tabSlot == WORK` the fourth slot reads Work and opens the Work space, which then carries the bar with Work current. With Search there, nothing changes. Search stays one tap from the top of Home in both states. The Work space has a shortcut row: Inbox · Projects · People · Organisations · Decisions · Commitments.
4. **Marks from the notification and lock screen.** `RecordingMarks` is now the single list of marks (the recorder screen reads it too, so a tap anywhere shows everywhere). For a work recording with other people in it, a second ongoing notification carries ⭐ Key · ✓ Action · ? Question. It's visible on the lock screen, says nothing about the meeting, and is removed when the recording ends. Each tap is turned into a mark at the recording's own clock (`MarkActions`) and is saved with the others via `Marks.save` when the recording is saved, including when it's stopped from the notification.
5. **Next-meeting widget** (`NextMeetingWidget`, `widget_next_meeting`): the next event, a live countdown, Record, and the prep line. On a home screen a sensitive profile gets "Your next meeting", a countdown and Record, with no names.
6. **Accent colours.** Look and home already had an accent token that works on both themes (Indigo, Teal, Slate, Green, Sky, Amber, Rose). I added **Emerald** and **Crimson**, so all five from §8.7 are there.
   - **Deep links wired:** `Prepare`, `RecordEvent` and `WeeklyReview` (placeholders from W11) now open the Prepare sheet, the recorder with title and workflow, and the review.

## Not built or changed

- **The accent is not stored in `AppIdentity`**, as §8.7 says. It's in `Appearance`, where the existing accent already lived. Adding a second copy would have made two sources of truth.
- **Quiet hours are "outside working days and hours"**, not a separate setting. `WorkSettings` has none, and the Faith quiet hours (22:00–07:00) are separate and unchanged.
- **"Starting now" always opens with the Meeting workflow.** The calendar doesn't say what kind of meeting it is. The person can change it on the recorder.
- **The plan covers items, not loose tasks.** Open commitments and questions are in it. Tasks with no commitment behind them are not.
- **The widget refreshes** when the 15-minute check runs (only if Prep or Starting now is on), when the app opens, and every three hours. A meeting added a minute ago may take a while to appear if none of those is on.
- **Mark taps that happen in a crash** are lost. The list is kept in memory until the recording is saved.
- **The Prepare notification opens the Work space** (which shows the sheet), not the person's current home style.
- **No end-to-end test of `WorkRhythmWorker`** (it reads the phone's calendar). Its decisions are tested in `WorkNotices` and `WorkWindow`, which is everything it calls.
- **No device check.** Nothing in W12 has been seen on a phone: notification layout, the mark actions on a real lock screen, the widget, and the bar.

## Risks / questions for the orchestrator

- **Two ongoing notifications while recording.** Android shows three actions at most, and the recording notification already has Pause and Stop, so marks needed their own. If you'd rather have one notification, Pause would have to give way to the marks.
- **Notification permission.** On Android 13+ nothing posts until the person grants it, which the app already asks for at startup. I haven't added a work-specific explanation.
- **Exact alarms.** The morning and weekly alarms are exact only if the phone allows it; otherwise they can be a few minutes late in Doze. That's the same as the Faith reminders.
- **Battery.** The 15-minute check runs only when Prep or Starting now is on, and it exits at once outside working hours.
