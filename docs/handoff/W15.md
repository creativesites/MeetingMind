## Built

1. **Automation Recipes Engine (`core/work/Recipes.kt`):**
   - Implements the strict flow: **Trigger → Context → Skill → Proposed Actions → Approval → Output** (PLAN_PROFESSIONAL.md §6.7, D8).
   - **Triggers:**
     - `RecipeTrigger.MeetingProcessed`: filters by workflow (e.g. `CLIENT_CALL`), project, or organization.
     - `RecipeTrigger.Schedule`: recurring time-based activations (e.g. Friday 16:00).
     - `RecipeTrigger.InboxItem`: triggers on newly captured items entering inbox.
   - **Skills:**
     - `MINUTES`: drafts formatted markdown meeting minutes with key commitments and decisions.
     - `FOLLOW_UP_DRAFT`: drafts follow-up email/message based on meeting commitments and attendees.
     - `CREATE_TASKS`: extracts commitment items into proposed project tasks.
     - `UPDATE_PROJECT`: generates summary note updates for the project notebook.
     - `BRIEF`: compiles weekly review briefs.
     - `REMINDER`: schedules follow-through and review reminders.
   - **Proposed Actions & Strict Approval Barrier:**
     - `ExportMinutesAction`, `DraftEmailAction`, `CreateTasksAction`, `UpdateProjectAction`, `ScheduleReminderAction`.
     - Output gathered into an `ApprovalCard` with `ApprovalStatus.PENDING`.
     - **Nothing outward and no state mutations execute until the user taps "Approve All"**.
   - **Built-in Recipes:**
     - **Recipe #1 (The Wrap-Up):** Triggered after any meeting; generates task drafts and follow-up draft.
     - **Recipe #2 (Client Meeting Follow-Through):** *"When a client meeting ends, draft minutes and the follow-up, create the tasks, update the project, and remind me Friday."*
     - **Recipe #3 (Friday Weekly Review):** *"Every Friday at 16:00, generate the weekly review brief and review commitments."*
   - **Privacy Gates:**
     - Evaluated against `WorkProfile`. Sensitive profiles (`CLINICAL`, `LEGAL`) automatically suppress unauthorized outbound draft creation.

2. **Recipe Preferences & Persistence (`core/work/RecipePreferences.kt`):**
   - DataStore persistence for active recipe toggles and per-step customization overrides (`disabledSteps`).
   - Requires zero Room database schema migrations.

3. **User Interface (`feature/settings/`):**
   - **`RecipesScreen.kt` & `RecipesViewModel.kt`:**
     - TopAppBar with navigation back.
     - Overview banner explaining automation and the strict approval guarantee.
     - Pending Approvals card view: allows reviewing proposed actions and tapping "Approve All" or "Dismiss".
     - Active Recipes list with master toggles.
     - Expandable recipe step customization: toggle individual skills (e.g. enable minutes and tasks, disable reminders).
   - Navigation:
     - `Routes.RECIPES` registered in `AppNavigation.kt`.
     - Settings screen navigates to Recipes via new "Recipes & Automation" row.
     - Wired in `MainActivity.kt`.

## Not built or changed

- **No visual automation builder:** Deliberately omitted per D8 and §6.7. Pre-configured recipes with toggleable skill steps provide high leverage without desktop builder bloat.
- **Zero unapproved background execution:** No automatic network dispatch or uncontrolled database writes.

## How to see it

1. Open **Settings** from the top bar or profile icon.
2. Under General settings, tap **Recipes & Automation**.
3. View the active recipes:
   - *The Wrap-Up*
   - *Client Meeting Follow-Through*
   - *Friday Weekly Review*
4. Tap **Customize steps** on any recipe to toggle individual skills on/off.
5. If any automations were triggered, review the proposed actions in the **Pending Approvals** card and tap **Approve All** or **Dismiss**.
