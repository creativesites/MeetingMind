## Built

Numbers follow the brief's W7 items.

1. **Entities and `MIGRATION_17_18`.**
   - New in `core/database/Items.kt`: `items`, `item_evidence`, `item_links`, `item_events`, `project_members` and `ItemDao`.
   - `people` gains `domainsJson`, `description`, `urlsJson`, `logoPath` and `propertiesJson`.
   - All eight indices from the brief are there, plus unique `items(sourceFindingId)`. I also added indices on `item_evidence(itemId)` and `item_events(itemId)`.
   - The migration SQL was copied from Room's exported `18.json`. `app/schemas/.../18.json` is committed.
2. **`core/work/Items.kt` (`ItemRepository`).**
   - Covers create, `setStatus`, `setDue`, `setOwner`, `setText`, `setProject`, `markReviewed`, `setTask`, `delete`, `supersede`, `answer` and `link`.
   - Every write logs an `item_events` row inside the same Room transaction. A change that changes nothing logs nothing.
   - Also has `ItemKind`, `ItemStatus` (with `allowedFor(kind)`), `ItemEventType` and `Direction`.
   - `isOverdue` and `isDueSoon` are computed, never stored.
3. **Promotion.** `core/work/ItemPromotion.kt`, called from `WorkRepository.confirm` through the new `promoteToItems`.
   - Decisions become `DECISION` and `ACTIVE`. A `SUGGESTION` decision that hasn't been reviewed becomes `PROPOSED`.
   - Unresolved questions become `QUESTION` and `OPEN`. Questions already answered in the recording come across as `ANSWERED`, with their answer.
   - Actions and follow-ups owned by someone else become `COMMITMENT THEIRS`. Actions I own become `COMMITMENT MINE`, linked to the task `confirm` made.
   - It is idempotent through `sourceFindingId`. On a repeat it brings in the person's Wrap-up edits and marks the item reviewed.
   - Evidence is copied from `sourceSegmentIdsJson`: segment ids, min start, max end, and a quote capped at 280 characters.
   - Links go to the meeting, note, project, organisation and participants.
   - `projectId` is the note's notebook. `orgId` is the notebook's `orgId` property, or else the single organisation shared by the participants.
   - A finding dismissed in the Wrap-up soft-deletes its item, if it had been promoted unreviewed.
4. **Commitment ↔ task sync.**
   - `ItemRepository.onTaskDone` is called from `TaskRepository.toggleDone` (through a new optional hook, wired in `TaskReminders.repository`) and from `WorkRepository.toggle`.
   - It is also called from `markFollowUpSent`, which closes follow-up tasks.
   - The reverse direction, completing or reopening a commitment, writes through to its task.
   - Loops are guarded by "already in that state" checks. A cancelled commitment is never reopened by a tick.
5. **Data migration**, run once from `WorkStartup` (new flag `items_backfilled_v1`) through `core/work/ItemBackfill.kt`.
   - Every work-type meeting's findings are promoted. It is reviewed if `reviewedAt != null`, otherwise unreviewed (`reviewed = 0`).
   - Each live `waitingOn = 1` task becomes a `COMMITMENT THEIRS` with `taskId` set.
   - If the task came from a finding that was already promoted, the existing item gets the `taskId` instead, so there is no duplicate.
   - Task rows are kept.
6. **Reads.**
   - `WorkRepository.observeDecisions` and `observeOpenQuestions` now read items and map to `FindingRow`. Same signatures.
   - New: `observeDecisionLog` (includes replaced decisions) and `observeCommitments(direction)`.
   - `WorkAllScreen` tabs are **You owe · They owe · Decisions · Open questions · People**. The enum names are unchanged, so routes still work.
   - **You owe** is my tasks plus any `MINE` commitment that has no task of its own. **They owe** is the `THEIRS` commitments.
   - "Waiting on" on the Work space and Professional home now counts `THEIRS` commitments.
   - Answering a question from the Work lists answers the item, and also marks the recording's own question resolved so main's screens agree.
   - The old DAO queries are untouched.

Two more things I did on my own:
- `WorkRepository.addTask` with a "waiting on" person now also creates a `THEIRS` commitment. Otherwise a task added by hand would vanish from They owe.
- `WorkRepository.toggle` and `snooze` accept a commitment's id (`item_…`) as well as a task id, for commitments that have no task.

## Not built or changed

- **`WorkPeople.merge` doesn't move items yet.** `ownerPersonId` and `counterpartyPersonId` on items keep pointing at the merged-away person. That is a gap against "correct once, fixed everywhere". It needs a small addition in W8 with the context pages.
- **The person and project screens** (`WorkPersonScreen`, `ProjectScreen`) still read tasks and main's decision and question tables. W8's context page replaces them, and the brief only asked for the Work lists to change.
- **The startup backfill covers work recording types only.** `GENERAL` and `CUSTOM` recordings are promoted when the person does a Wrap-up on them. Non-work users get no new rows on upgrade.
- **Unreviewed items are kept out of the You owe, They owe and Open questions lists**, matching what v34 showed. The Decisions list shows every non-superseded decision.
- **`counterpartyPersonId` is never set** at promotion. There is no reliable source for it yet.
- **The brief lists `UNCLEAR` commitments for W9**, so promotion never produces them.

## How to see it

- **On the phone:** Work space → tap **You owe**, **They owe**, **Decisions** or **Open questions**. The tab chips read You owe · They owe · Decisions · Open questions · People.
- **After upgrading from v34:** the first launch backfills items. Existing "Waiting on" tasks show under **They owe**.
- **Confirm a Wrap-up:** decisions, questions, and promises from both sides appear in the lists.
- **Tick or reopen a task** from either the Tasks screen or the Work space. The linked commitment follows.

Nothing in the UI shows evidence or the change log yet. That is W8 (context pages, Pulse).

- **The environment had no Android SDK at `/root/android-sdk`.** I installed platform 36 and build tools there from `dl.google.com`, and wrote `local.properties`, which is gitignored. Nothing in the repo changed for it.
- **Branch.** I used the assigned `ccr-417dd2a6-v88ts6`, not `pro/w7-w15`, as the brief allows.

- **Backup/restore is unchanged.** It copies the database file, so items ride along. I did not check restore of an older backup into schema 18 beyond the existing migration path.
