Meet Zuri, and friends.

Zuri means "beautiful, good" in Swahili. It is MeetingMind's companion and the brand name of the whole family. People choose a form (Zuri, Wren, Page or Nas the puppy) during onboarding or later in Settings, and can rename it. All four forms run on one shared rig and state machine and are drawn from simple primitives, so Compose can build them. They take any accent and work on Paper and Graphite. Below the forms: Create modes, a "3D look", Zuri as an assistant, and some wild ideas. Anything marked Idea is a brainstorm, not a commitment.

Create modes live outside the six app states and appear only when the user adds the companion to a card. The AI suggests a mode based on what the card says, and the user can change it. Question 1 · 3D and lifelike motion

How alive can Zuri feel on a mid-range Android phone?

Switch on 3D look in the controls bar. Every character on this page then gets rim light, a soft specular highlight, ambient occlusion and pointer parallax (gyro on phones). It's all done with layered gradients, which Compose Canvas can draw the same way. The hero on the left goes further: a real-time raymarched 3D Zuri in WebGL, to show where the ceiling is. Drag across it.

Real-time SDF raymarch: one fragment shader, no assets. The sprout follows the mic level. It renders only while on screen.

My recommendation: tiered, cheapest first

Ship Compose pseudo-3D now (what the 3D toggle shows) for every form and every size. Evaluate Rive only for hero moments, after P-2 ships and once we can measure it. Use pre-rendered 3D only for store art and Create export stills. No real-time 3D in the app. The WebGL hero is a reference for the feel, not a plan to ship it.

Tier 0 · 24–40 dpFlat vector, still

Rows, chips, the long-press target and the 24 dp glyph. It changes only on state changes, with a 150 ms crossfade and no idle animation.

Tier 1 · 56–120 dpCompose pseudo-3D

Home greeting, recording, processing, empty states. Layered radial gradients, rim, specular and contact AO. Parallax comes from scroll and touch; gyro only on screen and at about 30 Hz.

Tier 2 · 160 dp+ heroesRive, if it earns it

Onboarding hello, the first-recording peak, note ready. Rive adds secondary motion (ear flop, wing settle, squash) through a state machine with inputs for state, mic level and mode. If the size budget says no, Tier 1 already looks good.

Question 2 · Zuri as an assistant

From companion to assistant, without getting in the way

Companion vs assistant

Zuri is pulled, not pushed. The character stays small and quiet. The assistant appears when you ask (Ask Zuri, a long-press, voice) or at moments you scheduled yourself (a brief before a meeting, the Friday review). In an answer, Zuri is a small avatar at the top and the answer is the hero. Zuri never interrupts a recording and never writes into a note unless you tap "Add to note".

Private vs online

Every answer says where it was computed. On-device: local search over your notes plus the small local model. It answers with citations; short and slower. Online: Gemini for synthesis, drafts and briefs. Only the relevant snippets are sent. Prayer lists and Circles can be marked on-device only. When the notes don't contain an answer, Zuri says "I couldn't find that in your notes" and never makes one up.

Crazy ideas Ideas only

Wild ones, each with a one-line sanity check. None of them are commitments.

    Choice, made affordable

    Keeping four forms cheap and on-brand

    One rig, one state machine

    Every form takes the same Pose (offset, squash, tilt, eyes, look, mouth, brows, hop) from one function. Each form is only a drawing plus a few parameters. This page runs all four forms on one pose engine.

    One brand family

    "Zuri and friends." Copy says your companion's name; the system, the store and marketing say Zuri. Each form keeps Zuri's eyes and cheeks, so they read as one family.

    A neutral glyph outside the app

    Notifications, the widget and the launcher shortcut use the three-bar Zuri sprout glyph whatever form is chosen, so the brand stays consistent where it's seen most.

    Golden tests, bounded

    Per form: 6 states + 6 Create modes × 2 themes × (full, 24 dp) = 48 golden images. Four forms make 192. They are generated from one parameterised test, so adding a form adds data, not test code.

    Build order

    Zuri first (P-2, about 6–8 days). Each further form is about 3–5 days on the shared rig. Ship Zuri and Nas at launch and add Wren and Page as fast follows if the schedule is tight.

    Smart default

    Zuri is pre-selected for every space. A user who skips the choice gets the brand, the record-button family and the most faith-neutral form.

    New open questions

    For the founder

    1. When someone picks Nas, do notifications say "Nas" or "Zuri"? I suggest the user's chosen name in the message text and the Zuri glyph as the icon.
    2. Check the name "Nas". It is a well-known rapper's name, and in Arabic "an-Nās" ("mankind") is the title of the Qur'an's final surah. That's probably fine, but worth checking with your audience, as is the fact that some households consider dogs unclean.
    3. Launch set: all four forms at once, or Zuri + Nas first with Wren and Page as fast follows?
    4. Size budget: would you accept roughly 2–4 MB per ABI for Rive in Tier 2 hero moments, or should we stay pure Compose?
    5. What counts as a sermon? Should "Quiet during sermons" apply to recordings typed Sermon only, or to every Faith-space recording?
    6. Ask Zuri on Free: is on-device only (slower, shorter answers) acceptable, or is Ask a Pro feature?
    7. Live in-meeting whisper: is it on the roadmap at all, given recording-consent laws and how it would feel to the other people in the room?
    8. Create: should the companion ever be suggested by default on a card type (for example study wins), or always start off?
    9. Circles: can members see each other's companions (for example on a shared prayer list), or does the companion stay private to each person?
