MeetingMind · Companion · Phase 1: directions

Who should Mimi be?

Here are four directions for MeetingMind's companion. Each one is built only from circles, ellipses and a few short paths, so the Android build can draw it in Compose Canvas without Lottie or Rive. All four take your accent colour, work on Paper and Graphite, and run on the same six-state rig. Try the controls first. Then read the recommendation: I'd ship one of these characters, not let users pick from four.

Recommendation

Ship one Mimi, the Orb. Let people make it theirs with colour, presence and name, not by picking a species.

The Orb is the record button, awake. It is the only direction that is the product itself, it reads perfectly at 24 dp, it carries no faith or cultural baggage, and "Off" costs nothing because the face simply goes to sleep and the orb stays a button. The Wren is a close and lovable runner-up. The reasoning, and my honest position on letting users choose a character, are below.

My position

Should users pick their character?

Not at launch. I'd ship one Mimi with light customization, and I'd make the Orb that Mimi.

  1. A bond needs a someone, not a skin. Duo and Finch work because there is one of them. Recognition builds in notifications, the widget, store screenshots and word of mouth ("the little orb told me my sermon notes were ready"). Four characters split that memory four ways.
  2. It is a choice at the worst moment. Step 1 of onboarding comes before the user has seen any value. A mascot picker asks for taste before we've given anything, which works against reciprocity and adds choice overload. The choice people really need there is how present Mimi is, not what animal it is.
  3. Cost grows with every state, forever. Each character multiplies drawing × states × reduced-motion poses × the 24 dp simplified version × screenshot tests. That cost recurs: every future state (reading, proud, curious) and every seasonal touch is paid four times.
  4. The Orb makes "Off" honest and free. With the Orb, choosing no companion just means the face sleeps and the orb stays a plain record button. With any other character, "Off" leaves a hole in the layout that needs its own design.
  5. The Orb is the product. "A recording is the source." The object that listened is the one that cares about the result, and the wait while it processes reads as care (the labor illusion) with no extra metaphor.

Where the Orb is weak, and how to fix it. "A blob with eyes" is a common AI-assistant trope. Two things make it ours. The first is the three-bar sound sprout on its head: a waveform that is also a tuft of hair, and it gives the Orb a silhouette nothing else has. The second is personality carried by motion: the squash, the hop and the sprout dancing to your voice. One placement rule: the bottom-bar Record button stays faceless, so there is never any doubt that it records. Mimi wakes on the recording screen, and the shared element grows from the button.

If you still want choice later: add one alternate form, the Wren, built on the same rig and state machine. This file runs all four characters on one shared pose engine, which shows that a second form costs about +60%, not +100%. Even so, I'd only do it if testers ask for it.

Before Phase 2

Open questions for you

  1. Is "users select a character" a must-have, or would colour + presence + name meet the intent behind it? This decides whether Phase 2 designs one character or a rig for several.
  2. Face on the Record button, or not? I recommend that the bottom-bar button stays faceless and Mimi wakes on the recording screen. Do you want the face on the button itself?
  3. Does the name stay "Mimi" whatever the form, and can people rename it (Advanced)? And which pronoun should copy use: "it", "they" or "she"?
  4. Where are most of your testers? Owls (and some colours) carry strong meanings in parts of Africa, Latin America and the Middle East. If the Owlet is still in the running, I'd like to test it with a few Faith testers first.
  5. Faith default presence. Is it OK for Faith-space users to start on "Big moments", so Mimi stays quiet while a sermon is recorded and only shows up when the note is ready?
  6. Spark cards. Should people ever be able to add Mimi to a shared card, opt-in only? Or should Mimi never appear in shared content at all?
