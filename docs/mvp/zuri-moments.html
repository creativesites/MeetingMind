Three moments people remember.

Zuri listens, keeps, then gives it back and steps aside so your note is the hero. The first time, the handover is a small ceremony. After that it's a quick, varied unveiling that stays fresh at the 50th note. On a card, Zuri is a guest you invited. Press Play, slow it to 0.25× and scrub. Tap any phone to skip, just as in the app.

Moment 1 · the first-recording celebration

The First Keep

The first real note is ready, so the thing that listened hands back what it kept. It plays over the note screen, which is already rendered underneath, so Open note just dismisses the overlay. Once ever, skippable, and it never blocks. Switch the form to see each one's own take: Nas fetches it, Wren perches on it, Page becomes it. Choose the Faith space for the quiet version.

      Why it works

      Peak-end: the first note is the moment the promise is proven, so we design its peak. Labor illusion pays off: the same character that narrated real stages now hands over the result. Reciprocity: value with nothing asked; there's never a rating prompt or upsell here. Von Restorff: the only 160 dp bloom in the app, and it never repeats.

      Haptics: a tick at the apex and a confirm when the card lands. None in Faith. Sound: a soft chime at 1.5 s (decided: it ships), never in Faith, and with a "Sounds" switch in Advanced. This page is silent.

      Moment 2 · every later note

      The Unveil

      A note is unveiled once: the first time it's opened, within 24 hours. Zuri hands over the header, then leaves; it never stays on the note. Press Next unveil a few times to see the rotation: deterministic, never the same twice in a row, and weighted by the space at the top of the page.

        Moment 3 · Create card with Zuri

        The Card

        Off by default, always the user's choice. The AI suggests a pose and never touches the words. Disallowed poses are hidden, not greyed out. Zuri steps onto the card, does its mode's gesture once, then settles into exactly the pose that gets exported. Press Play, or switch on "Include".

        Format

          Export: a static image

          Idea, out for now A 2-second animated export (MP4, H.264, 30 fps): draw the card once and redraw only Zuri's rect for 60 frames, then MediaCodec + MediaMuxer. About 1.5–3 s to encode on a mid-range phone, ~0.4–0.9 MB, no new dependency. Animated WebP needs a native encoder (+300–500 KB per ABI), so it isn't worth it. Never offered for prayer requests.

          What these moments deliberately don't do

          Restraint is part of the design

          RuleWhy
          The note is underneath from frame 0Any tap skips and reaches what it landed on; Back skips; Share and Open never wait.
          No counts, streaks or guiltNo "50 notes kept" counter, no "you didn't record today". The 10th note is a Proud milestone, once.
          Nothing piggybacks on a peakNo rating prompt, upsell, paywall or share nudge after an unveil. "Share a moment" exists only on the First Keep.
          Never celebrate a failureFailed or partial notes get the honest line and a fix; on-device fallbacks lead with the truth.
          Sacred stays quietNo moment on prayer requests, grief or Good Friday. Faith has no hops. Sermons get a warm glow and a soft nod.
          Still afterwardsEvery moment ends in a still pose with the clock stopped (0 fps). No idle loops designed to pull attention.
