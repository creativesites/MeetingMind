Circles: a place people are glad to come back to.

An interactive redesign of the whole Circles experience on release/1.0-paid-launch, built on the code as it is today (feature/circles2, core/circles2, server/circles-api), the approved Circles plan and the product rules. Every phone is live: tap, type, press and hold, drag the catch-up cards. One world runs through all of them: you are Ana Mwansa on Tuesday 17 November 2026, early evening. You belong to New Life Fellowship (Grace, Daniel, Ruth, Michael, Esther and 12 more) and you own Acme Integration Team with Sarah Chen and James Obi from Acme.

Specs: design/circles-experience-design-spec.md (audit, architecture, components, privacy, backend mapping), design/circles-motion-spec.md (tokens and the full motion inventory) and design/circles-innovation-concepts.md (nine concepts, ranked). Tags on each frame: built exists today, restyled · improve exists, behaviour or layout changes · new UI new surface over data the app already has · needs Worker needs a server change you'd approve separately. Small grey NEEDS WORKER labels inside the phones mark the exact parts that depend on one.

1 · One shell, two homes

The identity header stays. Under it, a thumb dock (Home · Updates · ＋ · Chat · People) replaces the top tabs so everything is within one thumb. The first tab is where Faith and Work part ways: a living room for Faith, a status board for Work. Everything else is shared parts with different words and data.

2 · Participation, not engagement

The primary verbs are human: I'm praying, Going, Seen it, I can help, Mark sent. They work inline, where you see the request. No streaks, no rankings, no online dots, no red badges for chat chatter. Catching up has a definite end.

3 · Motion that explains

The cover you tap becomes the room you enter. A seat fills when you say Going. A prayer warms to gold when it is answered. Each animation has one job, uses a shared token, plays once, and has a still alternative. Use Slow ×5 above to study any of them.

4 · Honest by design

"Not end-to-end encrypted" is said where it matters. Unsupported files are refused before upload with a reason. A timeout never looks like an empty Circle. Anonymous means anonymous, even to leaders. Confidential meetings can't be shared, with no override.

What gets simpler

  • Create and Join appear once (＋), not four times.
  • Composer types appear once, inside the composer, after you start writing.
  • Ten cards above the first post become filters plus an optional draft line.
  • Save lives in the post footer only; Mark done only on the action.
  • Events leave the feed for Home sections and their own screen.
  • Settings stays a full page, reached from ⋯ with notifications beside it.

What the audit found (full table in the spec, §2)

Keep and restyle

Animated list search, space-aware covers (as ripple fields), reaction haptics, the gallery and video speed, saved posts, shared checklists, tags, quiz polls, event templates, categorised search, drafts everywhere, push routing, invite codes.

Improve

Threads (the model has parentId; the UI sends none), upload progress (indeterminate today) and Retry (missing), MOV/3GPP refusal (after reading the file, video picker only), Work's identical tab set (no Overview), the PDF hand-off to other apps.

Make contextual

Catch-up card and Today prompt become the catch-up deck. Poll, Card and Celebrate stay in the chat tray only. The Work pulse strip and next-sync strip move into Overview.

Proposed for removal (your call)

Daily Circle Pulse and streak on the list (its "Check in now" doesn't check in, and the approved plan says no streaks). Contribution counts and "Top contributor" on members. Members stats row. Each is listed in the spec with what replaces it.

Decisions for you

D1 · Thumb dock

Move the Circle tabs to a bottom dock with a ＋ in the middle. Recommendation: yes.

D2 · Streaks out

Remove the Pulse card and streak; add the opt-in Daily rhythm per Circle. Recommendation: yes.

D3 · No contribution ranking

Replace post counts and "Top contributor" with "you and Ruth" connections. Recommendation: yes.

D4 · Faith Home order

Devotional hero, then prayer, then gatherings, then testimony and reading. Or lead with prayer?

D5 · Worker changes

Approve separately: external member flag, decision status and "replaces", invite preview, event bring-list. Read together's anchored comments come later.

D6 · New behaviours

Turn chat into a post; type suggestions while writing (on-device keyword rules, no AI); in-app PDF reader. Recommendation: all three in this release.
