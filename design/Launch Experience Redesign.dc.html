Get people to their first real note, then sell what they now understand

This canvas pairs with design/launch-experience-audit.md, which holds the audit, the evidence and the file:line citations. Every phone below is live: tap buttons, chips and links. Frames are drawn at 360 dp, the narrowest phone we support, in the existing Midnight Graphite tokens (Inter and Outfit). Turns run in journey order.

Honesty rule for this canvas: every capability shown exists in the code today, or is marked needs build. Prices are the live Play Console prices; the app always renders whatever Play returns. Allowance numbers are the live proxy table (free 30 min and 30 AI actions, Pro 20 h and 1,200, Church 60 h and 4,000). In the app they are read from the proxy, never hardcoded.

1 · Zero downloads to first value

Internet mode is pre-selected and saved. On-device AI is a guided, optional setup after onboarding, never a gate.

2 · One model per job

Three plain jobs: Speech to text, Who spoke, and Summaries & Ask. The user's languages pick the speech model. Any extra download must state the job it does.

3 · Offer at the moment of need

No paywall in onboarding. A quiet card after the first successful note. Feature sheets when a locked feature is in hand. At the limit, "Process on this phone" sits beside "Upgrade".

4 · Settings in the bar

Today · Notes · ＋ · {your space} · Settings. Plan, AI and privacy are each in one place. Developer rows go to debug builds.

5 · Say exactly what leaves the phone

Each processing choice states where audio and text go, including the DeepSeek text backup. The Search privacy leak gets fixed first (audit §11).

TURN 1Onboarding: four screens, outcome first

Removed from onboarding: the companion pick (moves to a "meet your companion" card after the first note, D4), the outcomes and work-profile questions, name, Bible and reading plan (moved to Faith's first open), the model catalogue, and the bundled permissions. Every string is a resource, and the sample is translated too.

1aWelcome
  • A looping demo shows the outcome: voice → speakers → notes. No feature list.
  • The language pill stays (Bemba users choose before reading).
  • Two exits. "Record something now" skips straight to 1d.
  • Motion: transcript lines rise in sequence at 400 ms steps. Reduced-motion shows the final state.
1bWhat will you record?
  • One multi-select question replaces goals, outcomes, work profile and name.
  • It maps to spaces, as today, and picks the sample and paywall wording.
  • Skip keeps every space on.
1cSee it work
  • The existing sample, pre-set from 1b and now localised.
  • Speakers show as coloured names, so diarization is explained by seeing it, never by the word.
  • Tap "What did we decide?" to see a cited answer.
1dHow should MeetingMind think?
  • Internet is pre-selected and saved (D1). This fixes the current OFFLINE/INTERNET conflict.
  • Plain trade-offs: where audio goes, downloads, signal, speed.
  • "On this phone" never shows a catalogue here. It sets up after onboarding (2a).
  • "What's the difference?" opens a side-by-side sheet, including what always needs the internet and the DeepSeek text backup D10.
1eHome after onboarding
  • One job on screen: make your first note. Record, import, or replay the sample.
  • A 3-step getting-started checklist (dismissible) replaces the 6-stop coach-mark tour.
  • Shown only if "On this phone" was picked: a non-blocking "Finish on-device setup" card.
  • Settings is now in the bar (6a).
1fMicrophone, asked in context
  • Shown on the first record tap (or here on the Record-now route), right before Android's dialog.
  • Says where audio goes for the mode chosen in 1d.
  • Notifications are asked later, on the processing screen (4d), when there is something to notify about.

TURN 2On-device AI setup wizard

One path replaces today's two (Setup "three small jobs" and the AI engine catalogue). Languages pick the speech model, so the wizard and ProcessingScheduler follow one shared rule. Memory gates use the catalogue's existing minimumRamMb (today informational only). Example phone: 6 GB of memory, 41 GB free. All sizes are from ModelCatalog.kt.

2aWhy on this phone?
  • Honest scope: what works offline, what still needs the internet (YouTube import, live voice, AI images), and what it costs.
  • Entry points: the Home card, Settings → AI & privacy, and "Process on this phone" offers.
2bLanguages pick the speech model
  • Pre-filled from the app language and 1b.
  • The live hint says which model covers the selection, and when one model covers all of it.
  • No auto-detect claim: language ID isn't built (autoDetectLanguage has no reader).
2cYour setup: three jobs, one model each
  • The smallest useful set is recommended. Extras are opt-in and labelled with the job they add.
  • Silero VAD is folded into Speech to text: a helper, not a choice (fixes it appearing as a speech model).
  • Total size, free space after install, Wi-Fi only on by default (D7).
  • Tap "Change" on Summaries & Ask to see the comparison sheet.
2dNot enough space
  • Caught before download (the repository check exists today; the UI is new).
  • Offers the real way out: the Lightweight summaries model, or freeing space.
2eDownloading
  • Per-job progress over the existing WorkManager download (resume, SHA-256, retries).
  • "Checked and installed" means the checksum passed.
  • The user is never held here: "Use MeetingMind while this finishes".
2gInterrupted · waiting for Wi-Fi
  • Keeps the partial download (HTTP Range resume exists).
  • Says how much is left, and offers mobile data as an explicit choice.
2fReady: process here?
  • Finishing the downloads doesn't silently flip the mode. The user chooses.
  • Says where to change it later.

TURN 3On-device AI management

Replaces the "AI engine" list. It shows jobs, not a catalogue, and keeps the device strip and technical details one tap down. Every destructive action states its consequence. Removing a model never touches recordings, transcripts or notes (verified: models live only in filesDir/models).

3aOn-device AI
  • Storage bar, the three jobs with the active model, and "Also on this phone" with "Remove to free X".
  • A paused download shows inline Resume and Discard.
  • Tap Remove or Change to see 3c and 3d.
3dChoose a speech model
  • Tags: Recommended · Lightweight · Higher accuracy · Installed · Not suited to this phone.
  • Each row says which languages it serves, which explains the per-language switching that today happens silently.
3bReplace or keep both
  • No silent duplicates: a second model for a job asks "Replace" (default) or "Keep both", with the storage cost.
  • The old model is removed only after the new one verifies.
3cRemove, with consequences
  • Today the trash icon deletes immediately, with no confirmation.
  • The sheet says what stays, what stops working, and what still works in Internet mode.

TURN 4First recording, first result

Each first-time hint appears once, at the moment it is useful, and is dismissible. The recorder tells you in advance where processing happens and what it uses.

4aNew recording
  • Type, spoken language and number of speakers. Speaker count feeds diarization's expectedSpeakerCount.
  • A processing line shows mode and allowance left, with Change.
  • The first tap on Record shows the mic primer (sheet).
4cRecording, first time
  • A calm screen with one hint: Marker.
  • Stop is the largest control (80 dp). Marker and Pause are 64 dp.
4dProcessing + notification primer
  • Each stage is tagged Internet or This phone, so the privacy claim is visible.
  • The notification permission is asked here, the first time there's something worth notifying.
4eNotes + first-result value card
  • The upgrade moment is an inline card at the end of the first successful note, not a dialog 2.5 s in.
  • It only appears when notes completed from a real recording, never on failures or the sample.
  • The copy uses this recording's real length and the live allowance.
4fTranscript with speakers
  • A speaker strip with a one-time hint: "Tap a speaker to put a name to the voice".
  • Markers appear inline in the transcript.

TURN 5Paywall and upgrade moments

One scrolling page replaces the 3-page flow, with the price always visible in the button. Contextual sheets explain a single feature. At the limit, the free path ("process on this phone", "wait until 1 Dec") is shown as an equal choice. Frequency: at most one app-initiated prompt a week. Three dismissals of a kind silence it for 30 days. Taps the user starts never count.

5aLocked feature: Autopilot
  • Explains the one feature, with a preview, then names the plans that include it.
  • "Not now" returns to the note. It does not start the cooldown (today it mutes the first-result offer for 7 days).
  • "Pro and Church" assumes D6. Today BYOK includes Autopilot too.
5bPlans: personalised, one page
  • The headline follows 1b (this is the Meetings version).
  • "Free, what you have" comes before "Pro adds", with real numbers.
  • The launch-offer line appears only when Play returns the offer. The renewal date is spelled out.
  • Restore, Terms, Privacy and Other plans are always visible.
5dApproaching the limit
  • A quiet line on the recorder, not a dialog.
  • It says what happens to a long recording before it happens.
5eAllowance used
  • The recording is always saved.
  • Three honest routes: this phone (or set it up), upgrade, wait for the reset.
  • Today this is an "OK" dialog with no on-phone path needs build.
5fPlan & billing (Free)
  • Usage with the reset date (UTC month). Restore lives here too.
  • "What happens if I cancel?" answered in-app.
5gPlan & billing (Pro, payment issue)
  • Shows the grace state from EntitlementState.GRACE, with the fix in Google Play.
  • Manage opens Play subscriptions. No in-app cancel (Play rule).
5hPurchase results
  • Success returns to the feature that was asked for, now unlocked.
  • Use the buttons to see Failed (nothing charged), Pending (polls, as today) and Restore with nothing found.

TURN 6Navigation, Work and Settings

The "To review" overlap is a layout bug with a known cause. ProfessionalHome.kt:246 floats the tile up 38 dp into a header that reserved no room (showTile = false). Fix: put the tile in the list flow. Every bar uses equal-width items with one-line labels and 48 dp targets. Nothing grows a fixed-height chip.

Proposed: Settings takes the second space slot (D3)

Work bar today: Home + 5, selected chip grows (≈390 dp with "Decisions" at 360 dp)

Proposed Work bar: 5 equal destinations, Home moves to the header back arrow (D11)

6b0Work home today (reproduced)
  • Rebuilt from the code, not a screenshot: the 76 dp tile pulled up 38 dp over the mode caption.
  • Seven 48 dp quick tiles with clipped 11 sp labels. The Up next button is ≈36 dp tall.
6bWork home, fixed
  • Up next and To review are separate cards in the flow. No offsets.
  • Quick actions: 4 + More at 64 dp. "At a glance" is a 2×2 grid whose labels wrap.
  • Type from MM.type only. No 9.5–11 sp literals.
6cWork space with the new Work bar
  • The back arrow in the header returns Home. The bar holds only Work's own five places.
  • Labels stay one line. Bar labels cap their scale at 1.15× (Material practice) so they never fold.
6dSettings tab
  • The account card carries the plan and this month's usage.
  • 9 groups, each a single place: no row reachable from 3–4 places as today.
  • Developer rows (Gemini log, benchmark, test onboarding and paywall) go to debug builds only.
6eAI & privacy
  • The one place to switch processing, with a plain "what leaves your phone" panel for each mode.
  • Confidential Work projects always stay on the phone (WorkPrivacy, exists).
  • "Search stays on this phone" holds only after audit fix §11.1 privacy fix first.
6fStress: French at 1.3× text
  • The same Work home, with longer strings and larger type: cards grow, labels wrap, nothing overlaps.
  • Bar labels ellipsise only past 1.15×. Each item keeps a full content description.

TURN 7Empty, loading and error states

One pattern for all of them: what happened, whether anything was lost (almost always: no), and what you can do now. The free path is always among the actions.

7aEmpty Notes
  • Two real actions and a reminder of what MeetingMind makes.
7bLoading
  • Skeletons in the final layout, so nothing jumps when content lands.
7cNo connection (Internet mode)
  • Saved and queued, with the on-phone option.
7dTranscription failed
  • Plain cause, audio safe, retry or switch to this phone.
7eNo speech heard
  • The note still gets its moment (PRODUCT.md), and a likely reason is offered.
7fOwn key not working (BYOK)
  • Names the provider's answer, with no jargon. Two ways forward.
