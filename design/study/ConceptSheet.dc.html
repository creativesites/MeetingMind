Enzyme kinetics Reaction speed rises with substrate concentration, then levels off at Vmax, when every enzyme is busy. Km is the concentration that gives half of Vmax.

Km

Michaelis constant
Getting there

The substrate concentration at which the reaction runs at half its top speed. Lower Km, tighter grip.
