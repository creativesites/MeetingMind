Learning: turn what you listen to into knowledge you keep

A redesign of the whole Learning vertical for university students who record lectures. One scenario runs through every screen: Thandi, a second-year student, studying Human Biochemistry and its Enzyme Kinetics — Lecture 07 (competitive inhibition, Michaelis–Menten kinetics, enzyme activity and reaction rates). It is Thursday 13 November, 9:30.

How to review. Start with the walkthrough (turn 0): one live phone with journeys to launch, a note on every screen and switches for dark mode, large text, offline and “done for today”. Every frame below is live too: tap anything. Append #3a-style ids to the URL to jump to a screen. The written spec is design/learning-vertical-design-spec.md.

1 · Three places, plus a mode

Learn · Library · Progress sit in the space’s bar. Practice is a full-screen focus mode you enter from wherever you are, so it never becomes a fourth list of the same items.

2 · One bounded plan a day

Learn shows a single plan of about 14 minutes with one primary button. It has a real “done for today”, a 5-minute version, and no backlog that grows.

3 · The session mirrors the loop

Source · Understand · Practise · Mastery are four tabs of one workspace, with a “Next step” strip that is always one tap away.

4 · Evidence, not scores

Five qualitative states with a glyph, a label and a plain-language reason. There are no percentages or predictions. Every claim cites a timestamp, and unclear audio is flagged and held back.

5 · Retrieval before rereading

Answers are hidden until you try. The study guide ends in a quiz, not more text. Ask ends in “Quiz me”.

Deliberately not here

  • Teach-Back, Study With Me (1.1)
  • Exam dates, mock exams, readiness (R2)
  • Knowledge map, cross-source sessions (R3)
  • Streaks, leaderboards, sharing
